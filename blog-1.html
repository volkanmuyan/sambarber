How to Maintain Your Fade
Between Appointments

By Sam the Barber · June 2026 · 4 min read

A precise fade is one of the sharpest looks a man can wear — but it needs upkeep. The fade is a gradient, and gradients grow out unevenly. Here's how to keep yours looking fresh for longer.

1. Use the Right Products Daily

A lightweight pomade or styling cream can help define the shape of your cut and add a subtle hold that makes the fade look cleaner. Apply sparingly — a little goes a long way. Avoid heavy waxes that build up over time.

2. Keep the Sides Moisturised

The faded areas of your scalp are freshly exposed skin. Dryness and flakiness will draw attention to the fade line. Use a light scalp oil or moisturiser every 2–3 days to keep the skin healthy and the contrast sharp.

3. Sleep Smart

Cotton pillowcases create friction that can frizz up the top section of your fade. Switch to a satin or silk pillowcase, or wear a loose wave cap at night. Your morning styling routine will thank you.

4. Book a Touch-Up at Week 3

The sweet spot for a fade is between day 7 and day 14. By week 3, the gradient softens noticeably. A quick trim (not a full cut) every 2–3 weeks keeps the fade looking intentional rather than grown out. Ask Sam for a "shape-up" appointment — shorter, more affordable, and keeps you sharp.

5. Avoid Over-Washing

Washing your hair every day strips the natural oils that keep your scalp balanced. Aim for 3–4 times per week with a sulfate-free shampoo. Between washes, a dry shampoo can absorb excess oil without disrupting your style.

"The fade doesn't end when you leave the chair — it ends when you stop caring for it."

— Sam the Barber

When to Book Your Next Appointment

Every 2–3 weeks for a shape-up. Every 4–6 weeks for a full cut. Listen to your hair — some grow faster, some slower. If you're unsure, Sam will advise you on the ideal schedule during your visit.
