Beard Care Essentials:
Oils, Balms & the Right Routine

By Sam the Barber · May 2026 · 5 min read

A well-groomed beard is a signature. It communicates confidence, discipline, and style. But a neglected beard does the opposite. Here is the routine Sam recommends to every client who sits in his chair with a beard.

Step 1: Cleanse — Not Every Day

Beard hair is coarser than scalp hair, and the skin beneath it is more sensitive. Washing with a dedicated beard shampoo 3 times per week is ideal. Every day is too much — it strips the sebum that naturally conditions both the skin and hair. Between washes, rinse with warm water only.

Step 2: Beard Oil is Not Optional

Beard oil is the single most important product in a beard care routine. Applied after washing (or after a warm shower), it replenishes moisture, softens the hair, and prevents the dreaded "beard itch" that comes from dry skin beneath. Apply 3–5 drops to your palms, rub together, and work through the beard from root to tip. Focus on the skin underneath.

For dry skin: argan or jojoba oil. For oily skin: lighter options like grapeseed or sweet almond oil.

Step 3: Balm for Shape and Hold

If your beard is longer than 3cm, a beard balm is your best friend. Unlike oil, balm offers light hold and helps train the hair to grow in the direction you want. Apply a small amount (the size of a fingernail) after your oil, working through the beard and shaping with a boar bristle brush.

Step 4: Brush Daily

A boar bristle beard brush does two things: it distributes the oils you've applied evenly through the beard, and it exfoliates the skin beneath to prevent ingrown hairs. Brush downward first to detangle, then style as desired. Two minutes a day makes a visible difference within two weeks.

Step 5: Professional Trim Every 3–4 Weeks

Even if you're growing your beard out, a professional trim every 3–4 weeks removes split ends, shapes the neckline, and ensures even growth. An untrimmed beard grows unevenly — some hairs grow faster than others. Sam's beard sculpt service takes care of all of this in one session.
