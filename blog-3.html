The Hot Towel Shave:
Why This Ritual Is Making a Comeback

By Sam the Barber · April 2026 · 6 min read

In a world of five-blade cartridges and electric shavers, the hot towel shave might seem like a relic. But across Belgium — from Brussels to Aalst to Leuven — men are rediscovering what their grandfathers knew: there is no better shave than the one done by a skilled barber with a straight razor and a hot towel.

What Is a Hot Towel Shave?

A hot towel shave is a full-service shave performed by a barber. The process begins with a warm towel applied to the face for 2–3 minutes. The heat opens the pores and softens the beard hair, making it far easier to cut cleanly. A rich lathered shaving cream — applied with a traditional brush — is then worked into the skin. The barber shaves with a straight razor in precise, controlled strokes, following the grain of the hair. A second towel, this time cooler, closes the pores afterward. A moisturising balm finishes the ritual.

Total time: 30–45 minutes. The result: the closest, smoothest shave you've ever had.

Why It Disappeared — and Why It's Back

The hot towel shave fell out of fashion in the 1970s as disposable razors became cheap and abundant. Why pay a barber when you could shave at home in five minutes? The answer, it turns out, is experience. The rise of wellness culture — the same movement driving cold plunges, saunas, and slow mornings — has made men reconsider what their grooming routine actually does for them beyond the functional.

A hot towel shave is not just grooming. It is 40 minutes of being cared for. Of stepping away from screens and stress. Of coming out looking visibly better and feeling genuinely restored. That is worth something.

The Skin Benefits Are Real

Beyond the ritual, the hot towel shave delivers measurable skin benefits that a home razor simply cannot. The straight razor exfoliates as it shaves, removing dead skin cells and revealing the layer beneath. The hot towel opens pores and reduces the likelihood of ingrown hairs. The cool towel afterward tightens the skin and reduces post-shave inflammation. Men with sensitive skin or persistent razor burn often find that switching to professional hot towel shaves eliminates the problem entirely.
