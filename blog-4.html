Haar & Baard:
The Perfect Combination

By Sam the Barber·May 2026·4 min read

A fresh haircut alone can look great. A well-trimmed beard alone can look great. But the two together — aligned, balanced, and shaped by the same hand in the same session — creates something else entirely: a complete look that turns heads.

Why the Haircut and Beard Must Be Done Together

The relationship between your hair and beard is one of proportion. A long beard needs a certain amount of hair on top to balance it. A short fade calls for a tighter beard line. When you do them separately — haircut at one place, beard at home — you risk an imbalance that no amount of product can fix.

At Sam the Barber, the Haar & Baard package (€30) is designed so that Sam cuts the hair first, then shapes the beard in relation to the new haircut. The neckline, the fade level, the beard outline — all calibrated together, in real time.

What's Included in the Haar & Baard

The session typically runs 45–60 minutes and covers:

Haircut: Precision scissor or clipper cut, including the top, sides, and neckline. Adapted to your face shape and hair texture.

Beard trim: Full beard sculpting with clippers, scissors, and a precision liner. The cheek line, neck line, and mustache are shaped and defined.

Finishing: Light product application and styling to complete the look before you leave the chair.

The Proportions Rule

Sam uses a simple principle: the tighter the haircut, the sharper the beard line. Skin fades and close crops call for very precise beard edges — think razor-sharp lines. Looser, textured cuts allow for a softer, more natural beard shape. During your session, Sam will advise on the beard shape that best complements your haircut, face structure, and personal style.

"The beard and the haircut are one decision, not two. That's why we do them together."

— Sam the Barber

Add a Wash for the Full Experience

If you want the complete grooming session, the Haar, Baard & Wassen package (€35) adds a professional hair wash before the cut. This softens the hair for a cleaner scissor cut, removes product buildup, and — frankly — feels incredible. Many clients consider it essential rather than optional.
