Kinderen — Kids Cut:
Making the Chair Fun

By Sam the Barber·May 2026·3 min read

A child's first barber experience sets the tone for a lifetime of grooming habits. Done right, it becomes something they look forward to. Done wrong, it can create anxiety around haircuts for years. At Sam the Barber, we take that responsibility seriously.

Patience First, Everything Else Second

Kids move. Kids get distracted. Kids sometimes change their minds mid-cut. Sam is experienced with all of it. The kids cut session is unhurried by design — no pressure, no rushing. If a child needs a moment to settle, we take that moment. The result is always worth the extra care.

The Kinderen service is priced at just €15 and is available at both our Aalst (Dirk Martensstraat 1) and Leuven (Savoyestraat 3) locations. Suitable for boys under 12.

What to Expect During Your Child's Visit

Before the cut: Sam will ask the child (not just the parent) what they want. Kids respond better when they feel heard. If they have a style in mind, Sam will work with it. If they're unsure, he'll suggest something age-appropriate and easy to maintain.

During the cut: Calm music, no sudden movements with clippers near sensitive areas, and a relaxed pace. Sam uses a light clipper guard to avoid any discomfort, and scissors are used for the top to keep things gentle.

After the cut: A mirror moment for the child to see their new look — that reaction of pride and excitement is the best part of the session.

Tips for a Stress-Free First Barber Visit

Time it right. Don't bring a hungry or tired child. Mid-morning after breakfast is usually the best time.

Talk it up positively beforehand. Frame it as an adventure, not a chore. "You're going to get a cool haircut like Dad" lands better than "you need a haircut."

Bring a favourite distraction. A small toy, a book, or the parent's phone with a favourite video can help keep the child still during the cut.

First visit? Book a quiet slot. Avoid busy weekend mornings. Midweek sessions are calmer and give Sam more time and space to make the experience relaxed.

"A kid who enjoys their haircut becomes a kid who takes pride in how they look. That's the start of good grooming habits."

— Sam the Barber

Popular Styles for Boys

The most requested styles for boys at Sam the Barber are the classic school cut (short back and sides, tidy top), the textured fringe (longer on top, scissor-cut natural texture), and the skin fade (for older boys who want something sharper). Sam will advise on what works best for your child's hair type and face shape.
