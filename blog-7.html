Epileren — Threading:
The Precise Art of Hair Removal

By Sam the Barber·April 2026·4 min read

Threading is one of the oldest forms of hair removal in the world — and one of the most precise. Using only a doubled, twisted cotton thread, an expert technician removes hair at the follicle level with unmatched accuracy. At Sam the Barber in Aalst and Leuven, threading is available for just €10.

What Is Threading?

Threading (epileren in Dutch) uses a thin cotton thread twisted into a double strand. The thread is rolled across the skin in a rapid motion, catching and pulling hair from the follicle. Unlike waxing — which removes a layer of skin along with the hair — threading affects only the hair itself. This makes it far gentler on sensitive skin and ideal for the delicate areas around the eyes, upper lip, and chin.

The technique originated in South and Central Asia thousands of years ago and has been practiced continuously ever since. It requires significant skill and practice, but when done by an experienced practitioner, it is fast, precise, and remarkably comfortable.

Threading vs. Waxing: Why Threading Wins

Precision: Threading can remove individual hairs or precise rows of hair with surgical accuracy. Wax removes everything in a patch — including hairs you didn't want to remove.

Skin safety: No chemicals, no heat, no risk of burns or allergic reactions to wax. Threading is completely safe for all skin types, including those on retinoid treatments or other topical medications that make waxing unsafe.

Longevity: Because threading removes hair at the follicle level (like waxing), results last 3–6 weeks. Regrowth is finer and softer over time with repeated treatments.

Speed: A full eyebrow threading session takes 5–10 minutes. No waiting for wax to set, no strips to pull, no cleanup.

What Can Be Threaded?

At Sam the Barber, threading is offered for all facial areas: eyebrows (the most popular), upper lip, chin, cheeks, and forehead. Eyebrow threading is by far the most requested — the precision of the technique makes it the gold standard for eyebrow shaping.

Many clients combine threading with a haircut or beard service to get a complete, polished look from head to eyebrow. The €10 threading service can be added to any appointment.

Does It Hurt?

Threading involves a sensation similar to tweezing — a brief, sharp pull as each row of hair is removed. Most clients describe it as "more intense than expected but completely manageable." First-timers often report that by the second visit, the discomfort is significantly less as the skin adjusts. The sensitivity of the area affects the sensation — eyebrows are mild, upper lip is more noticeable.

After the treatment, the skin may appear slightly pink for 15–30 minutes. This fades quickly and is completely normal. Avoid direct sun exposure for a few hours post-treatment.
