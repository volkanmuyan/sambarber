Keratin & Permanent:
Transform Your Hair

By Sam the Barber·March 2026·5 min read

Some transformations happen with a pair of scissors. Others require chemistry. The keratin treatment and the perm are two of the most powerful hair treatments available — one eliminates frizz and adds smoothness, the other creates lasting structure and curl. Both are available at Sam the Barber, starting from €60.

The Keratin Treatment: Smooth, Manageable Hair for Months

A keratin treatment is a semi-permanent hair smoothing procedure that infuses the hair shaft with keratin protein — the same protein your hair is naturally made of. The treatment seals the hair cuticle, dramatically reducing frizz, increasing shine, and making the hair significantly easier to style.

The results last 3–6 months depending on your hair type and how you maintain it. During this period, your daily styling routine is reduced to a fraction of its usual time — many clients report going from 30 minutes to under 5 minutes to achieve the same style.

Who Is the Keratin Treatment For?

The keratin treatment is ideal for people with naturally frizzy, curly, or unruly hair who want a smoother, more controllable result without permanently changing the hair's natural texture. Unlike chemical straightening, keratin doesn't break the hair's bonds — it coats and smooths them. This means the effect gradually fades back to your natural texture rather than leaving a harsh line of regrowth.

It's also an excellent option for people with colour-treated or damaged hair, as the keratin actually strengthens and repairs the hair shaft as part of the process.

The Perm: Lasting Curls and Texture

A perm (permanent wave or permanent haar) uses chemical processing to restructure the hair's bonds and set them in a new pattern — typically curls, waves, or volume. Modern perms are a far cry from the harsh, tight ringlets of the 1980s. Today's techniques create natural-looking waves, beach textures, and body that maintain themselves with minimal daily effort.

Results last several months, gradually relaxing over time as the hair grows out. The perm is an excellent choice for people with naturally straight or flat hair who want texture and movement without daily styling.

The Process: What to Expect

For keratin: The treatment begins with a thorough cleanse to remove all product buildup. The keratin solution is then applied section by section and worked through the hair. After processing time (30–45 minutes), the hair is blow-dried straight and then sealed with a flat iron at high heat. Total time: 2–3 hours depending on hair length.

For perm: Rods are placed in the hair according to the desired curl pattern. A chemical solution is applied to restructure the bonds, followed by a neutraliser to lock in the new shape. Rods are removed and the hair is styled. Total time: 2–4 hours. After a perm, the hair should not be washed for 48–72 hours to allow the bonds to fully set.

Post-Treatment Care

For keratin: use sulphate-free shampoo, avoid saltwater and chlorine for the first few weeks, and sleep on a silk pillowcase to protect the smoothness. For perm: deep condition weekly, avoid heat styling in the first week, and use a curl-defining product to enhance the shape.
