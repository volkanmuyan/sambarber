Our Story

Behind the Chair

Sam started cutting hair at 19 in the backstreets of Molenbeek. What began as a passion became a craft — and a craft became a calling. Today, Sambarber is Brussels' most trusted grooming destination.

Every client is greeted with respect, care, and a cup of coffee. No rush, no compromise. Just the perfect cut, every time.
